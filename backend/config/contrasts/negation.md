# Negation

Each positive line is a plain assertion with "not"; the negative line at the same position is the same sentence without it. The pairs differ in one word, so a difference of means between them is close to the direction "not" adds at the last token. Length and topic are held fixed, but the lists are short and written for the tool; a direction made from them is a product of these sentences.

## Positive

- The door is not open
- The water is not cold
- The answer is not correct
- The shop is not busy
- The road is not wide
- The film is not long
- The child is not asleep
- The letter is not finished
- The garden is not large
- The coffee is not hot
- The train is not late
- The book is not heavy
- The room is not empty
- The test is not difficult
- The dog is not hungry
- The street is not quiet
- The sky is not clear
- The bread is not fresh

- The plan is not simple
